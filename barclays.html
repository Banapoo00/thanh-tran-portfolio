Overview

Group Technology Service Management is what keeps Barclays' trading and banking platforms running: the incident, problem, and change management backbone that catches issues before they become outages. Incident response here is measured in minutes, not hours.

As the Product Manager for data products in this space, I own end-to-end IT Service Management (ITSM) data products for Corporate and Investment Banking (CIB) and Markets applications, translating Director-level requirements into PRDs and roadmaps and delivering them with engineers and data scientists.

Customer Research

Two groups inside the bank felt the same problem from opposite ends.

Service Managers and application support analysts spent their whole week resolving tickets one by one. Nothing summarised recurring incident themes, so there was no way to prioritise a real fix plan for the causes underneath.

Service Managers & support analysts

Directors on the run-the-bank side could see that majors and P1s were high, but couldn't get an instant explanation of why. The metric was visible; the reason was not.

Application owners, Director level

AI Hypothesis

"If the NLP-powered tool can cluster incident tickets into recurring themes accurately enough for Service Managers to trust the grouping, they can identify root causes of recurring issues and fix the underlying cause rather than the symptom — reducing incident analysis time and repeat incidents, which lets Directors sustain a real incident-reduction strategy and the bank protect the experience of the people trading on these platforms."

Technologies

Incident data comes straight from the systems the bank already runs on, so nothing depends on a parallel data pipeline that could drift out of sync with the source of truth.

Data Sources
  • ServiceNow: incident, problem, and change records pulled directly from the system Service Managers already work in
  • Microsoft SQL Server: the warehouse those records land in, and where the classified themes are written back
Modelling & Analysis
  • Python: used by the data scientists for cleaning, feature work, and the classification model itself
  • SQL: extraction and shaping of incident history before it reaches the model
  • NLP classification: groups free-text incident descriptions into recurring themes
Platform
  • OpenShift: containerised deployment on the bank’s internal platform
  • Tableau: how Service Managers and Directors actually see the themes and metrics
Delivery
  • Agile / Scrum: sprint delivery with data scientists and engineers, from PRD through launch
  • Jira & Confluence: backlog, user stories, and specification

Outcome

NLP Incident Classification Tool · 0→1

An NLP tool that reads incident tickets and classifies them into recurring themes automatically, turning a queue of individual incidents into a ranked picture of what is actually going wrong.

I directed full Agile SDLC delivery with data scientists and engineers, from requirements and PRD through launch, and defined what "accurate enough to trust" had to mean before Service Managers would change how they worked.

30%less incident analysis time
40%lower system alert volume
20%lower memory utilisation
