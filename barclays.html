Customer Research

Two groups inside the bank felt the same problem from opposite ends.

Service Managers and application support analysts spent their whole week resolving tickets one by one. Nothing summarized recurring incident themes, so there was no way to prioritize a real fix plan for the causes underneath.

Service Managers & support analysts

Directors on the run-the-bank side could see that majors and P1s were high, but couldn't get an instant explanation of why. They could see the number, not the reason.

Application owners, Director level

AI Hypothesis

"If the NLP-powered tool can cluster incident tickets into recurring themes accurately enough for Service Managers to trust the grouping, they can identify root causes of recurring issues and fix the underlying cause rather than the symptom, reducing incident analysis time and repeat incidents, which lets Directors sustain a real incident-reduction strategy and the bank protect the experience of the people trading on these platforms."

User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

Three roles, three different jobs to be done. Service Managers needed themes they could act on. Application owners needed enough detail to investigate. Directors needed enough context to make a call quickly.

How the Clustering Works

Free-text incidents are clustered by the terms that keep appearing together, then surfaced as themes sized by how often they recur, so the biggest block is the one to look at first. Clusters below the threshold stay unsurfaced.

Technologies

Incident data comes straight from the systems the bank already runs on, so there is no second pipeline to keep in sync.

Data Sources
  • ServiceNow: incident, problem, and change records pulled directly from the system Service Managers already work in
  • Microsoft SQL Server: the warehouse those records land in, and where the classified themes are written back
Modeling & Analysis
  • Python: used by the data scientists for cleaning, feature work, and the classification model itself
  • SQL: extraction and shaping of incident history before it reaches the model
  • NLP classification: groups free-text incident descriptions into recurring themes
Platform
  • ServiceNow: where the themes are surfaced, inside the system Service Managers already work in
  • OpenShift: containerized deployment on the bank’s internal platform
  • Tableau: Directors’ reporting layer, used to compare the NLP themes against existing central reporting
Delivery
  • Agile / Scrum: sprint delivery with data scientists and engineers, from PRD through launch
  • Jira & Confluence: backlog, user stories, and specification
